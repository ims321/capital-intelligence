Platform principles

Legal authority first

A willing lender is irrelevant if the borrower cannot lawfully borrow. Authority is checked before matching.

Stage-aware capital

Preparation grants are first-class. Construction capital is matched only when the project is ready.

Evidence over claims

Mandate ≠ available money. Every claim carries source, date, confidence and status.

Two-sided liquidity

Borrowers and capital providers both define needs. Matching is bidirectional and transparent.

End-to-end workflow

Discovery → interest → NDA → data room → terms → close, with full audit trail.

Neutrality

No paid ranking. Capital providers cannot buy preferential placement.

    How the provider database grows

    1) Seed from public mandates, annual reports and DFI/ECA programme pages — tagged “Mandate match” until confirmed. 2) Import in bulk via CSV (columns: name, category, type, ticket, currency, geographies, sectors, stages, description, eligibility, source). 3) Self-register — providers submit mandates themselves and move to “Verified” after review. 4) Licensed data (e.g. PEI, Preqin, IJGlobal, Convergence) plugged in for funds, pension and family-office coverage. 5) Every record keeps source, date and evidence status; stale records (>12 months) are flagged for re-verification.

    User guide

    How to use the platform as a borrower or capital provider. This guide reflects the prototype; production behaviour will be richer.

    1. Getting started

    Use the role switch in the header to choose Borrower or Capital provider. Tabs and available actions change accordingly. Select your jurisdiction — this loads the relevant legal and fiscal rules.

    2. Borrower journey

    • My projects — Register a new project or open an existing one. Start from a simple concept; the platform structures it.
    • Project workspace — Walk through lifecycle stages. See stage-matched capital and the bankability gap register.
    • Legal & fiscal — Check borrowing authority, approval paths, foreign-currency rules and guarantee constraints for your jurisdiction.
    • Financing scenarios — Compare whole-of-life structures, including FX stress.
    • Matches & engage — Review scored capital matches. Express interest or request an NDA.
    • Deal rooms — Manage NDA-gated engagement, data rooms and progress toward terms and close.

    3. Capital provider journey

    • My mandate — Define instruments, stages, ticket size, geographies and sectors. This drives opportunity matching.
    • Opportunities — Browse African projects that fit your mandate. Readiness score and legal status are visible.
    • Pipeline — Track engagements you have started.
    • Deal rooms — Same controlled workflow as borrowers once an NDA is in place.

    4. Capital intelligence

    • Capital explorer — Search the provider database by category (DFIs, pension funds, private equity, family offices, endowments, sovereign wealth funds and more), stage, instrument and evidence. Add providers or bulk-import by CSV.
    • Capital flows — Tabular view of illustrative flow patterns and drivers (“why capital goes where it goes”).
    • Flows map — Interactive world map. Arcs appear only when you click a country, showing inbound and outbound flows. Animation is optional and off by default.

    5. Evidence labels

    Every material claim carries a status: Verified current, Published programme, Open funding call, Historical precedent, Mandate match, or Inferred/analytical. Treat inferred and precedent items as leads, not commitments.

    6. AI wizard

    Click the teal assistant button (bottom-right) for guided help. The wizard can explain where you are, suggest next steps, and walk you through common tasks. It is decision support only — not a legal or investment opinion.

    7. Important limitations

    • All data in this prototype is illustrative.
    • Legal checks are decision support, not legal opinions.
    • No real funding commitments or live availability are shown.
    • Match scores are simple heuristics for demonstration.
